Please find attached the revised version of our manuscript, “How Many People Live Near Protected Areas in Developing Countries? Estimates from Gridded Population Data (2000–2020)” (reference ERL-124325).

We thank Referee 3 for the careful and constructive final comments. In response, we have extended the analysis of strict versus non-strict protected areas by adding a boundary-length-based standardization, alongside the area-based comparison already introduced in the previous revision. This additional diagnostic leads to the same substantive conclusion: non-strict protected areas remain associated with larger nearby populations, mainly because they are more numerous and collectively larger.

We also revised the treatment of the GHSL-WorldPop comparison. The manuscript and Figure 3 now state explicitly that, for the inside-PA perimeter, WorldPop estimates are systematically higher than GHSL estimates, whereas agreement is much closer for the broader inside-or-within-10-km perimeter. This asymmetry is now discussed in the text, while noting that its precise source cannot be established from our data alone.

In addition, we incorporated the reviewer’s suggested clarifications by adding the missing percentage for the 2000 inside-PA estimate, reporting the percentage of protected areas with missing designation year, and tightening the wording in the Discussion where requested.

The manuscript, supplementary materials, and generated outputs have been updated and re-rendered successfully. A detailed point-by-point response is provided in the accompanying response note.
